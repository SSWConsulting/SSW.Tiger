# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

SSW employees browsing generated meeting intelligence reports.

## Product Purpose

Provide one authenticated entry point listing all published projects and their meetings. Preserve existing report passwords and existing meeting URLs through a redirect from dashboards to portal.

## Positioning

The Portal is the user access layer for the existing TIGER transcript processing pipeline.

## Brand Personality

Use the existing SSW branding and clear English interface copy.

## Design Principles

Reuse published reports. Keep project-to-meeting navigation direct. Do not imply personal meeting filtering, roles or video features in this release.
